Every call provably lawful. Or provably refused.

An outbound collections agent that cannot dial until every legal precondition is satisfied. Five rules, evaluated per account against the recipient's own local time. Blocked calls never reach a provider, live opt-outs are honoured mid-call, and every decision — allow or refuse — is written to a tamper-evident log.
