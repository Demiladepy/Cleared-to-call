What this is not

Stated plainly, because an overclaim here would be the costly kind.

Legal advice
No. An engineering encoding of documented requirements.
Sufficient
No. ALLOW is necessary for this policy, not proof a call is lawful.
Complete
No state rules, frequency caps, or reassigned-number checks.
Guessing
Never. A missing timezone or consent record is a refusal.
